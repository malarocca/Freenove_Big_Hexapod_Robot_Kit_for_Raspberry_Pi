## Phase Goal

**As a** hexapod operator, **I want to** put the robot into an autonomous walk-and-avoid mode and take control back instantly at any moment, **so that** it can move around the house on its own without crashing into things, pets, or kids.

<objective>
Stand up the Walking Skeleton of autonomy: the complete `toggle → decide → sense → act → arbitrate → self-halt` pipeline, end-to-end, on real hardware, driven over the existing TCP command protocol.

After this plan the robot genuinely walks by itself, stops for what it sees in front of it, hands control back to a human instantly, and switches itself off after five minutes. It is not yet clever — it only looks straight ahead and it only stops (no head sweep, no evasive turn); those are plan 03. But every structural piece the rest of the milestone builds on exists and has been proven on the physical robot.

Purpose: this is the load-bearing safety foundation. All three of this codebase's known hazards (unsynchronized `command_queue`, dead TCP reconnect path, `gpiozero` smoothing that cannot report "no echo") sit directly underneath the safety claims made here, so all three are fixed in this plan rather than deferred.

Output: a new `Code/Server/autonomy/` package, a `CMD_AUTO` wire command, a manual-preempt arbitration gate, an interruptible `run_gait`, and the D-01 reconnect fix.
</objective>
