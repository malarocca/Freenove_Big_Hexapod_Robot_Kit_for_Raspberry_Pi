## Phase Goal

**As a** hexapod operator sitting at my desktop, **I want to** flip the robot into and out of auto mode with one clearly labelled button and always see at a glance whether it is currently driving itself, **so that** I never have to guess whether the robot is about to move on its own.

<objective>
Put a real, dedicated Auto Mode control on the desktop client and make the auto-mode status permanently visible.

Plan 01 made the robot autonomous over the wire. This plan makes it operable by a human at the client, which is what AUTO-01 and AUTO-04 actually require. The badge is deliberately driven by the server's `CMD_AUTO` ack rather than by the click, so it stays truthful when auto mode ends for reasons the user did not initiate — a five-minute timeout, an arrow-key preempt, or a fail-safe stop.

Purpose: this is the user-facing half of the Walking Skeleton. Without it the autonomy engine is a lab curiosity.
Output: `Button_Auto` and `label_Auto_Status` widgets, their wiring, a connection guard, and a `CMD_AUTO` client-side protocol constant.
</objective>
